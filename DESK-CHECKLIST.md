# Subtitles at the desk: one page

Print this and keep it next to the vMix PC. On a normal day you do not start, restart or fix
anything on the PC. The subtitles start by themselves when the PC's user signs in, and the
**overlay 2** button on **Captions Overlay** in vMix is the only control.

## Before katha

1. **The PC is switched on and signed in** to the usual Windows account. Give it a minute.
   Nothing else to open for the subtitles: they are already running, with no window.
2. **Open vMix** and load the katha preset. (If you open vMix before the PC has finished
   starting, it is fine: see "If the screen is blank" below.)
3. **The mixer send that feeds the PC is up and not muted.** A muted send looks exactly like a
   quiet speaker: the subtitles have nothing to hear and the screen says nothing.
4. **Press overlay 2** on **Captions Overlay**. The subtitles start by themselves a few seconds
   later.

## What a healthy screen looks like

- Two lines of white English text on a dark, see-through panel across the bottom of the hall
  screen.
- A small **AI-generated subtitles** label at the bottom right.
- A new line appears a few seconds after the swami speaks, and the older line rolls up.
- Gaps in singing are normal. A short pause while it reconnects is normal too.

## During katha

- To **stop** the subtitles, take overlay 2 off. They stop about 10 seconds later. A quick
  flick off and on does not stop them.
- **Do not close any window** to "tidy up", and do not update or restart the software on a
  katha day.
- Closing or restarting vMix does not break the subtitles program. When vMix is back, press
  overlay 2 again.

## If the screen is blank with overlay 2 on

1. **Wait one minute.** The software puts the page back by itself, usually within 45 seconds.
   Do not keep pressing the button.
2. Still blank after two minutes? On the PC's browser open **http://localhost:8765/**.
   - **The page does not open, or shows a red *No connection* banner:** the subtitles program is
     not running. Double-click **start-captions.bat** in the live-captions folder, wait half a
     minute, and then **leave its window open** (do not close it, and do not press a key in
     it). Then call the person below.
   - **The page opens but the level bar stays flat while someone is speaking:** the PC is not
     hearing the hall. Check the mixer send from step 3 above first.
   - **The page opens and the level bar moves, but the hall screen is still blank:** call the
     person below.
